# Products

OLOS is a personalized life operating system that aims at helping users live an intentional life.

# Dashboard

OLOS Dashboard will be a future project to provide a simple home dashboard of all apps
in the OLOS ecosystem.

# Finance Tracker

The Finance Tracker App was created in April 2026, before the inception of this project.
The goal of the app is to provide a central location to monitor all financial activities of a user.

# Journal

The Journal App is the primary tool provided by the OLOS ecosystem to help users life intentionally.
Through morning and evening reflections, users will set goals and reflect on their progress.
