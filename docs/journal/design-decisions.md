# Design Decisions

## Morning Entry Workflow

### User is automatically entered into new entry workflow

I envision myself waking up in the morning and while in bed, completing an entry.
By routing immediately into the morning entry workflow,
the friction to enter a new entry is reduced.

### User can skip morning entry

The app provides the option to skip most of the morning entry.
This feature is intended to be used when the user decides to take an intentional rest day.
Or they are in a rush and not have time to enter a full morning entry.

### Morning Greeting page elements

This page prompts the user to enter their mood and sleep times.
I want to track this data for my record keeping.

By bringing awareness to these things, I hope to improve my sleep consistency,
and to notice trends in my moods.

### Signal-to-Noise Concept

This was a concept I was introduced to in
[Kevin O'Leary talking about working with Steve Jobs on The Diary of a CEO podcast.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mpAZehPviLQ&t=535s).
I've since adopted this philosophy and decided to bake it into this journaling app.

### Prompting user for "Potential Obstacles & Expected Responses"

This is a design choice aligned with the way I work best.
The goal here is get the user to envision sources of frustrations in their day,
and prepare plans for what to do if frustrations occur.

This is follows a guiding principle of this app:
Progress is consistency.

### Reviewing Agenda

After establishing their priorities for the day in the previous section,
the user will be prompted to allocate time for those priorities.

This is follows a guiding principle of this app:
Define direction and take action.

### Morning/Evening questions are displayed one-at-a-time

The intention is to narrow the user's focus to one question
and avoid overwhelming them with multiple questions listed at once.

This is follows a guiding principle of this app:
Reduce friction and complexity.

## Evening Entry Workflow

### Morning/Evening Workflows are entered automatically after a customizable time

The settings page will provide the ability to customize the time at which they will
automatically be entered into the new entry workflows.
This allows the app to be adaptable to a user's lifestyle.

This is follows a guiding principle of this app:
Reduce friction and complexity.

### Signal items have 3 possible states at End Of Day (EOD)

At the end of the day, a priority can either be completed or incomplete.
The app provides a third option of "Push to Tomorrow".
By providing this option, the Signal item will be displayed to the user
in tomorrow's morning entry, ensuring they won't forget about it.

This is follows a guiding principle of this app:
Reduce friction and complexity.

### Habits are displayed before questions

They entire list can be dispalyed in a quick overview.
Habits exist in two states: complete, incomplete.

Prompting the user for to decide between two states for every item on the list
is less cognitive load than asking users to write a short response to answer a question.

User should always be prompted for Habits *before* questions.

This is follows a guiding principle of this app:
Reduce friction and complexity.
