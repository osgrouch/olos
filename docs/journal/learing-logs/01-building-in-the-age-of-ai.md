# Building with AI is weird

8/3/2026

We are in a very weird age for creativity.
Is it truly my work if I've had AI help me refine it?
I defined the concepts of this app but when I stumble into a problem,
I go to AI to get a second opinion. Nothing that I will build here is 100%
my own intellectual property. For I owe some of it to the AI models
that I get helpful feedback and ideas from.

Would I not be a fool if I did not adapt this technology into my workflow?
The world is moving towards AI integration more and more everyday.
If I don't adapt, I'll be useless in my own industry. Sucks for creativity,
but hooray for stockholder profits and productivity maxxing.
