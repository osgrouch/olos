# Operations

> *What am I continuously responsible for?*
>
> One Reminders list per Operation, inside the `OLOS` group. Reminders holds the
> Tasks; this file holds why the Operation exists and what it supports —
> Reminders cannot express support, and support is the point.
>
> Operations have no end dates. Archived, never completed. Archiving leaves
> children pointing back at it; deleting would mean resolving every child first.

## \<Name\>

**Responsible for** — one sentence.

**Looks neglected when** — the observable symptom. Checked in a MonthlyReview
instead of guessed at.

**Supports** — Outcomes this ladders up to, or "maintenance" if none.

**Status** — Active | Archived
